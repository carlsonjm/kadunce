# Accepted baseline — 2026-09-10

- Persistent tray safety switch: left-click toggle, right-click repair/settings,
  graphical-session startup, live registration checks, offline compatibility repair.
- Configurable Active gutter, 6–48 px, default 10 px; cached settings updates.
- Shared window restoration, manual window-operation release, fullscreen release
  correction and bounded source-size normalization for previews.
- Consistent Card Line corner mask; Active keeps the system rendering path.
- Two-group overview: 64% center, 54% neighbor, directional shoulder continuity.
- Native dock input passes through without selecting the old center card.
- New applications settle at center, then expand into Active.
- Versioned Tette integration, exact application identity matching and direct
  replacement of the guest center by the launched application.
- Tette guest footprint: 64% with zero/one neighbor, 54% with two or more.

The final guest-launch handoff and preceding visual/function changes were accepted
in live testing. Native tests, source guards, tray package tests and live safety
control checks pass. Test checklists remain available for future regression work.

Known follow-ups remain in KNOWN-ISSUES.md and WINDOW-STATE-TEST-PASS.md: broader
output/window-lifecycle coverage, constrained-window policy and existing-window
activation motion. This freeze does not claim those deferred tasks are complete.

System-wide rounded corners are an independently maintained effect, not bundled
into Kadunce. No package-update hook or automatic repair is included.
