ShuffleTable

Pull the top edge and your workspaces come down as tabs. Slide along them to look. Pull deeper and the chosen one's cards hang below it. Lift to go.

Active = this window
Spread = these windows
Table = these workspaces
Nothing on the monitor in this workspace

Two axes, one stroke

Down picks the level: workspaces, then cards.
Across picks the item on that level.
Back up to the edge cancels.

Lift

On a tab
That workspace, as you left it.
On a card
That card, Active in its workspace.
At the edge
Back where you were. Nothing changed.
After a pause
Rest on a card and it lifts. Push back up to the tabs, choose one, let go.

Try

  1. Press the top edge and pull down a little. Slide sideways across the tabs.
  2. Keep pulling past the dashed line. Slide sideways across the cards.
  3. Rest on a card until its line fills, push up to another tab or +, and let go.
  4. Flick down and let go: the tabs stay, and tap or hover works like a menu bar.
  5. With it open: ← → ↓ ↑ Enter Esc. Double-tap a tab to rename it.

Settled with J · 25 Sep

  • Top edge down opens Table. The same gestures work in every state.
  • Dropping a card on + makes a workspace. Its name is the label system.
  • Empty workspaces dissolve on their own unless pinned.
  • A workspace spans every display. There's one Table, on the display that holds the dock.
  • Tapping a card makes it Active in its own workspace.
