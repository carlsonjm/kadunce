Re-explain your previous message for a smart person who does not read code.

- Open with what it means for someone using Shuffle, or what decision it
  forces. Not the mechanism.
- Give one analogy from design, physical objects or everyday tools.
- Remove every technical term you can. Any that must stay, define once in
  plain words.
- Drop file names, symbol names and counts unless they are needed to act.
- Make it shorter than the original. If it is not shorter, it is not plainer.

Do not apologize or announce that you are simplifying. Just give the
explanation.
